### Demo and API

- On a phone (narrower than 600px) the browser bar is the address box and
  the menu button alone: Back, Forward, Reload, Saved pages, the extensions,
  the service worker's status, Shields and the star are gone from it, and
  from the tab manager's cards. The menu opened from the bar has them at its
  top, as Chrome's menu on Android does: a row with Back, Forward, Reload
  (Stop while the page loads) and the star, then Saved pages and Shields
  with the page's blocked count. The address box's text now runs up to its
  end when nothing sits over it.
