### Demo and API

- The demo shows a site's tab on the frame after the press that opens it,
  however it opens: a page the address's fragment names (a reload, a
  restored session, the link in a new tab) is in the browser view from the
  first paint, where the home screen used to show until the controller was
  up; a bookmark opened from the sidebar no longer slides it out over the
  page; and a link in the tab manager's dock closes the manager at once,
  where its card grew to full screen under a hidden address bar. Opening
  and closing the sidebar or the manager by themselves still animate.
