### Fidelity

- A base-uri or form-action violation says where its code was as Chrome
  does: the page's script (without its query) and the position of the
  call that set or inserted the base or submitted the form, the markup's
  base by the document's URL and the line and column after its start tag,
  and nothing for a form into a new window. The browser's named the
  inject's hook or the shell's markup, with its query, and the inject's
  own named nothing (csp-base-uri, csp-navigation).
