The epoxy transport (/epoxy/ in this file: index.mjs, epoxy-simd.wasm,
h3/stomp_h3.js and h3/stomp_h3_bg.wasm) is Fracital's fork of Mercury
Workshop's epoxy-transport, built with epoxy-tls and the patches in
deploy/epoxy of powerMouse's source.
